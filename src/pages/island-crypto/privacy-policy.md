# Privacy Policy — Crypto Island

**Last updated: August 27, 2026**

## 1. Who we are

**Crypto Island** (the "App") is published by **Lucas Rouret**, an individual developer (no company is registered at this time; a legal entity is planned for the future). No public business address is provided at this stage for personal security reasons.

For any question about your personal data: **rouret.lucas@gmail.com**.

Data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): Lucas Rouret.

No Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed, as this is not currently required given the scale of the processing involved. Any request regarding your data should be sent to **rouret.lucas@gmail.com**.

## 2. What the app does

Crypto Island displays the real-time price of cryptocurrencies selected by the user, sourced from third-party exchanges (Hyperliquid and other supported exchanges), and can surface this price on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island ("Live Activity"). A paid tier ("Pro") unlocks more exchanges, more cryptocurrencies, and background Live Activity updates.

**The app does not provide investment advice of any kind.** Prices shown are for informational purposes only.

The app is distributed worldwide, including in France, through the App Store.

## 3. Data we collect

### 3.1 Data you provide

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Apple sign-in identifier (Sign in with Apple) | If you create an account | Identify your subscription reliably, independent of any single device |
| Email address (if you choose to share it via Apple) | If you create an account | Account-related communication, where applicable |

Creating an account is **optional**: the app's free tier works without one. An account is only useful to carry your Pro subscription across devices.

### 3.2 Data generated by using the app

| Data | Where it's stored | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Live Activity push token | Supabase database, hosted in Ireland (EU) | Lets our server update your Live Activity in the background (Pro subscribers only) |
| Exchange / cryptocurrency you're tracking, linked to the push token | Supabase database, hosted in Ireland (EU) | Send the correct price data to your device |
| Subscription status, purchase history (App Store receipt) | RevenueCat (see §5) | Verify and persist your Pro subscription |
| Display preferences (tracked crypto, currency, exchange, onboarding step) | Locally on your device (`UserDefaults`), **never transmitted** | Remember your preferences |

The application server that relays prices and push notifications stores no personal data; it is hosted in **Switzerland**.

### 3.3 What we don't collect

The app does not request access to your camera, location, contacts, or photos — none of these permissions are declared in the app. There are no social features (sharing, comments, messaging).

## 4. Legal basis for each processing activity

- **Performance of a contract**: providing the Pro service, managing the subscription, authentication.
- **Consent**: enabling Live Activity notifications (explicitly requested during onboarding).
- **Legitimate interest**: service security, fraud prevention on in-app purchases.

## 5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell any data. Some data is shared with technical service providers (processors under the GDPR), who process it on our behalf and under our instructions:

| Provider | Role | Data involved | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apple Inc. / Apple Distribution International Ltd.** | Authentication (Sign in with Apple), distribution (App Store), push notifications (APNs) | Apple identifier, push token | Ireland (EU App Store transactions) and United States (underlying services) |
| **Supabase Inc.** | Backend hosting, authentication, database | User identifier, push token, tracking preferences | Ireland (European Union) |
| **RevenueCat, Inc.** | Subscription and in-app purchase management | Pseudonymous user identifier, App Store purchase data | United States |
| Application server host | Hosting the backend server (price and push relay) | No personal data stored; push token relayed in transit only | Switzerland |

Exchange platforms (Hyperliquid, etc.) receive **no personal data**: the app only reads their publicly available prices.

## 6. International data transfers

Your data hosted on Supabase stays within the European Union (Ireland). The application server is hosted in Switzerland, a country recognized by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection.

RevenueCat, Inc. and certain Apple Inc. services are based in the United States, so your pseudonymous purchase and authentication data may be transferred there. These transfers rely on the safeguards recognized under the GDPR (standard contractual clauses and/or the EU-US data protection framework in force at the time of transfer).

## 7. Data retention

- Account and related data: kept for as long as the account exists, deleted within **30 days** of a deletion request.
- Push token and price-feed subscription: automatically deleted when the corresponding Live Activity ends or the Pro subscription expires.
- Billing data (held by RevenueCat / Apple): retained under their own policies and legal obligations.

## 8. Your rights

If you live in the European Union, under the GDPR you have the following rights over your personal data:

- **Right of access**: obtain a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Right of rectification**: correct inaccurate data.
- **Right to erasure**: request deletion of your account and associated data.
- **Right to data portability**: receive your data in a structured format.
- **Right to object**: object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Right to withdraw consent** at any time (e.g., notifications).

To exercise these rights, contact us at **rouret.lucas@gmail.com**. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or your local supervisory authority.

If you live outside the European Union, you may have equivalent rights under your own country's law (for example, the CCPA in California). We aim to honor these in the same way — contact us at the address above.

## 9. Security

Traffic between the app, our server, and our providers is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). Database access is restricted through strict access rules (Row Level Security on the Supabase side).

## 10. In-app purchases

Purchases (monthly subscription, lifetime purchase) are processed by Apple through the App Store and synced via RevenueCat. We never have access to your payment card details.

## 11. Children

The app is not directed at individuals under 16 years of age (or the applicable digital consent age in your country, if different) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Any material change will be notified to you within the app before it takes effect.
