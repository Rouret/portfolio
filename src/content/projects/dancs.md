# DanCS

DanCS replays Counter-Strike 2 demos in 2D and 3D, with what every player saw and heard. Next step: reading strategies and spotting the mistakes an opponent can exploit, with AI models trained on pro demos.

- **[Website](https://www.dancs.app/)**
- Under construction, Windows installer coming

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **[Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org/)** - Demo parsing and replay engine
- **[React](https://react.dev/)** - Viewer interface
